About WaW FX Viewer

      

        
An unofficial tool made for the World at War modding community.

        
Browse the installed Modtools effects by eye and play supported visual previews. Find a filename, pause or restart playback, and adjust the playback speed.

        
Choose separate dark, grey, light or checkerboard backgrounds for thumbnails and the viewport. The app automatically chooses a view and distance, keeping the authored effect settings. Some large effects use a labelled close-up that shows only part of the effect. Effects with lights can show a simple light receiver. Sounds, editing, exports and the in-game environment are not included. Unsupported or incomplete effects are left out; Catalogue information shows the counts. Game files are never modified.

        
Independent of Treyarch, Activision and UGX. Built with JavaScript, Vite, Rust and Tauri. Open source under GPL-3.0; see ATTRIBUTION.md for credits.
